# TMail Web

A page where customers who bought a streaming service account read the mail that service sends to the account's login address.

## Language

**Seller**:
The person who sets up service accounts on TMail inboxes and sells them.
_Avoid_: Owner, admin

**Customer**:
The person who bought a service account outright and now owns it, including its inbox.
_Avoid_: User, renter, member

**Service account**:
A login on a supported service whose login address is an inbox.
_Avoid_: Account email, account (on its own)

**Inbox**:
An address on a TMail-hosted domain that receives one service account's mail. Its customer can read it but not delete or send from it.
_Avoid_: Mailbox, account email, email (for the address)

**Supported service**:
A streaming service whose mail the app shows, recognized by sender address.
_Avoid_: Provider, platform

**Verification code**:
A one-time code inside a mail that the customer types into a supported service.
_Avoid_: OTP, PIN, code (on its own)
